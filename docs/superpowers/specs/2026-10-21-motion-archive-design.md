# Motion Archive design

Add one immersive motion chapter after selected work. The user's fourteen supplied clips form the visual material; preserve existing portfolio facts and project priority. The chapter is a realtime WebGL world with curved media surfaces, a moving camera, a kinetic sculpture, and depth; it must never read as a flat video player. Scroll, click, keyboard, and horizontal touch gestures navigate the world. A complete index makes every clip discoverable. Decode only the selected muted video and show lightweight poster textures for the rest. Unmount the canvas out of view and respect reduced motion. Use descriptive labels rather than claiming authorship of the supplied clips. Keep the existing heroine scale and project sections intact. The old basic VRM study remains in source but is removed from the main page because its visual quality conflicts with the hero and new world.
